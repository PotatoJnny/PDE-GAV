The public task documentation describes the equation, geometry, accessible
inputs and scoring metric. Read the public README and its linked tool and solver
interface guides in the resources directory supplied by the Controller.

The current program, previous round notes and recorded results are available
when provided by the run. The environment exposes the Case acquisition and
evaluation tools enabled in this run; `agent_tool status` lists their current
availability. Continuous Cases support queries for the public initial condition
and forcing at coordinates you choose. Evaluation feedback supports development.

Relevant original papers, numerical-method references, documentation and public
implementations may be useful. You may consult them and choose how to use your
research time. Keep useful findings and source links in the round notes.
